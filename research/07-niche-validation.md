# 07 — Passionate-niche validation: FPV vs keyboards vs MTB (2026-09-01)

Method: 16 searches by a research agent. Purpose: which
"subreddit-grade" community best fits physics-designed printed
performance parts. (Later superseded by the pivot away from FPV; kept
as comparison data.)

## FPV drones
- Size: r/fpv and r/Multicopter ~300–350K each pre-2025; IntoFPV, Oscar
  Liang, large Discords. Racing-drone segment ~$260M (2026); broader
  FPV ~$1.2B (2024) → $3.7B (2032); hobbyists dominate aftermarket.
- Vibration obsession: jello, gyro noise, propwash; canonical
  soft-mount guides; 2026 guides on gyro noise floor analysis; TPU
  mounts $8–13 (up to ~$30); RDQ/GetFPV TPU sections; Etsy custom
  variants.
- Blackbox culture: PIDtoolbox, Plasmatree, fpvpidlab (31 modules).
  Precedent: Chris Rosser's AOS RC frames designed from blackbox data +
  resonance simulation, sold via mainstream retailers. Nobody offers
  "upload log → custom-printed isolation mount." Caveat: soft mounts
  can't fix arm-borne/mechanical noise.

## Mechanical keyboards
r/MechanicalKeyboards >1M; market ~$2.0B (2026) → $3.1B (2030);
acoustics obsession (gasket mounts, foam stacks, tape mods); vendors
Thock King, StupidFish ($4–25 kits). Pain is aesthetic, not measurable;
boards ship pre-dampened.

## MTB inserts / saddles
r/MTB ~444K. CushCore Pro $150/pair; Tannus $40–53. Printed-lattice
precedent: Specialized Mirror ($325–390), Fizik Adaptive (£300) —
proves WTP for architected damping, but low purchase frequency,
subjective performance, entrenched incumbents.

## Dark horses
Pickleball paddle cores (Wilson × Azul 3D lattice cores: Quiet Paddle,
Custom Core; USA Pickleball certification barrier); RC cars (damping
already adjustable); audio DIY isolation (Sorbothane, premium niche).

## Verdict
FPV wins (passion × repurchase × measurability × physics fit); MTB
second on WTP; keyboards third. Later research (files 15–16) found the
drone ladder ends in defense and the user deprioritized it.
