# 04 — Frontier: "AI designs it, machines make it" (2026-09-01)

Method: 14 searches by a research agent. Purpose: tier the path from
generative design to machine-directed fabrication; anchor the
thought-experiment top rung honestly.

## Tier A — deployed / commercial
- Lila Sciences: $200M seed (Mar 2025) + $350M Series A (Sept–Oct 2025,
  NVentures, In-Q-Tel, ADIA) ≈ $550M; mid-2026 talks ~$8.5B. "AI
  Science Factories"; independent validation thin.
- Periodic Labs (ex-OpenAI/DeepMind, GNoME authors): ~$300M seed (2025).
- Chemify (Cronin): chemputation; $50M+ Series B (Oct 2025); first
  Chemifarm facility; Gates TB grant (Dec 2025).
- Emerald Cloud Lab / CMU Cloud Lab ($40M): remote execution; human
  designs experiments.
- Divergent (DAPS): strongest commercial proof — $290M E (Sept 2025,
  $2.3B), revenue >5x 2025, 600+ part numbers, USAF $46B EWAAC support.
- AlphaChip: RL placement in shipped TPU generations, MediaTek adoption;
  reproducibility fight (Markov; ISPD 2023) vs DeepMind rebuttal (arXiv
  2411.10053); Nature investigation favored authors; margin contested.
- Relativity Space: retreated from fully-printed ideology (Terran 1
  retired); Terran R hybrid, 2026 debut; Eric Schmidt control 2025.
- Construction robots: FBR Hadrian X, ICON — single-machine, niche.

## Tier B — lab-demonstrated
- A-Lab (Berkeley): Nature Nov 2023 claimed 41/58 novel materials in
  17 days; UCL critiques (2024); Nature Author Correction Jan 2026 —
  36 of 57, "novel" removed from title. Loop real; verification
  overstated. Canonical lesson: closing the loop is easier than
  verifying its output.
- GNoME (DeepMind, Nature 2023): 2.2M predicted crystals; Cheetham &
  Palgrave (Chem. Mater. 2024): essentially none examined credibly
  novel-and-useful.
- MatterGen/MatterSim (Microsoft, Nature Jan 2025, open): TaCr2O6
  measured 169 GPa vs 200 GPa target (<20% error) — genuine first, n≈1.
- Coscientist (CMU, Nature Dec 2023), ChemCrow, 2025 multi-agent
  chemists — benchmark-scale.
- MIT CBA (Gershenfeld): discrete lattice assembly by robots made of
  the same voxels (Nat. Commun. Eng. 2022); Oct 2025 (arXiv
  2510.13686) meter-scale hierarchical assembly. Lab-scale.
- Swarm construction: Harvard TERMES (Science 2014) still canonical.
- Atomically precise: Zyvex Labs STM hydrogen depassivation lithography
  (single-atom precision on Si:H; DOE STTR with ORNL); Silicon Quantum
  Computing — Nature Dec 2025: 11-qubit processor from individually
  placed phosphorus atoms, fidelities 99.1–99.99%, improving with
  scale. Real atom-by-atom manufacturing — 2D, one substrate, devices.
- DNA origami: 10–100nm programmable structures; scale-up/cost
  bottleneck (JACS Au 2025 review).
- Northwestern (Kriegman): AI-designed walking robot in 26 s, printed
  (2023); Mar 2026 "legged metamachines" (PNAS) — closest end-to-end
  design→fabrication loop, toy scale.
- 4D/multi-material: BMF microArch M150 (25µm, SMPs + conductive
  elastomers) sold; 4D applications research-grade (IOP roadmap).

## Tier C — plausible, speculative
- Mechanosynthesis/assemblers: unresolved since Drexler–Smalley; DFT
  toolset feasibility; a few single-bond SPM manipulations; no
  assembler path. HDL is the only funded experimental lineage.
- Programmable matter at scale: scaling laws undemonstrated beyond
  dozens of modules.
- Stellar engineering: star lifting (Criswell, Fogg), Shkadov thruster
  (1987), Caplan thruster (Acta Astronautica 2019; ApJ 2020 constraints)
  — all Kardashev-II budgets (~4×10²⁶ W; humanity ~10¹³ W, K≈0.73).
  Induced stellar COLLAPSE: essentially no serious literature; requires
  ~solar masses or removing degeneracy support — ~20+ orders of
  magnitude beyond Tiers A/B. Standard-setting thought experiment, not
  a roadmap item.

Cross-cutting: deployed frontier is macro-scale (Divergent, AlphaChip);
lab frontier is closing verification gaps; atomic frontier is real but
2D/device-specific; ~$1.2B+ into Lila/Periodic/Chemify/Divergent in 2025.
